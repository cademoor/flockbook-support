Flockbook

A flock book that stays on your phone.

Support

Questions about the app can come to cade.moorman@gmail.com.

Privacy

The flock book lives on your phone. You can use Flockbook without an account. Birds, eggs, reminders, care records, and photos stay on that phone unless you choose to sync.

Sign-in is optional. Continue with Apple, Google, or Facebook sends that provider's name and email address to Flockbook so the account can be created. Sync then sends the book on that phone, including portraits, to the Flockbook server so it can come back on another device. Flockbook does not sell this information, does not show ads, and does not track you across other companies' apps.

Portraits use the camera or your photo library. Reminders can ask to send alerts. Those alerts stay on the phone.

Deleting the app deletes the book on that phone. Save a copy from the app when you want one off the device.

To delete the account and the copy of the book stored for it, sign in at flockbook.net/account or in the app and choose Delete this account. The book already on the phone stays until you delete the app. You can also email cade.moorman@gmail.com from the address on the account and ask for it to be deleted.
